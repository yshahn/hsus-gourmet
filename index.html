Our Story

In the Heart of Downtown Atlanta
Since 1989

Hsu's Gourmet serves the best Szechuan and Cantonese style cuisine the city has to offer. Specializing in serving only the finest and freshest Chinese cuisine, we've been a Downtown Atlanta landmark for over 35 years.

Located on the corner of Peachtree and International Boulevard, we're the perfect destination for business lunches, family dinners, and special occasions.
